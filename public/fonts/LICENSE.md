# Fonts

`shadows-into-light.json` holds single-stroke (centerline) glyph data for the
**Shadows Into Light** typeface, as distributed with Vara.js
(https://github.com/akzhy/Vara, MIT License, © Akash Kumar).

The Shadows Into Light typeface is © Kimberly Geswein, licensed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1 (https://openfontlicense.org).
